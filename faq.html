Feeding & transitions

Absolutely. Black soldier fly larvae (BSFL) protein is hypoallergenic, highly digestible, and approved for companion animal feeding by AAFCO and EU regulators. We tailor amino acid and fatty acid profiles to match the biology of each species, including rumen-protected ruminant blends, joint-focused equine mashes, and calcium-balanced turtle bites.

Transition gradually over 10–14 days: mix 25% BSFL formula with your current diet for the first three days, increase to 50% for the next four days, then 75% before reaching 100%. Sensitive pets may benefit from a 21-day transition.

Start with 0.25 lb per head per day blended with your existing mineral or TMR program. Over 7–10 days increase to the recommended rate on the label, ensuring free-choice access to clean water and monitoring rumen fill, cud chewing, and milk solids. Because our ruminant blends are microencapsulated, they bypass the rumen and should not disrupt forage digestion when introduced gradually.
