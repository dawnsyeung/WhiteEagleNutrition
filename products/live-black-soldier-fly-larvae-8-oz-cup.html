Live Black Soldier Fly Larvae 8 oz cup

Give your animals a naturally nutrient-rich treat with live black soldier fly larvae from White Eagle Nutrition. These active feeder larvae are a high-value supplemental treat for chickens, ducks, quail, reptiles, fish, wild birds, and other insect-loving animals. Packed fresh in a ventilated 8 oz container, our live BSFL are easy to feed, easy to store, and naturally appealing to animals that thrive on whole insect nutrition. They provide a rich source of protein, healthy fats, calcium, and natural insect-based compounds that support a varied, species-appropriate diet. Use them as a training reward, enrichment treat, foraging supplement, or occasional high-protein snack.
