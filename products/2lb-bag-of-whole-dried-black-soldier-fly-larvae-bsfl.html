Whole Dried Black Soldier Fly Larvae (BSFL)

Premium Protein. Naturally Sustainable. Irresistibly Nutritious. Give your animals a clean, high-value protein they instinctively crave. Our Whole Dried Black Soldier Fly Larvae (BSFL) are gently processed to preserve nutrients, flavor, and crunch—making them a powerful, sustainable addition to everyday feeding routines. Raised in Canada, our BSFL are rich in protein and beneficial fats. BSFL support strong growth, energy, and feed efficiency across a wide range of animals—all with a fraction of the environmental footprint of traditional feeds. Why BSFL? High-Protein, Whole-Food Nutrition – Naturally dense in protein and fats without fillers or additives.
